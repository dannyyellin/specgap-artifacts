 judge_model:     LLM that did the matching (gpt-5.4 or claude-sonnet-4-6)
 experiment_type: microservices or restestbench
 app:             app name (nutrition, public-library, pet-store, or RESTestBench app)
 variant:         specific variant, e.g. auth-R1R2R3
 detector_model:  LLM that ran gap detection
 method:          zeroshot / oneshot-tracker / oneshot-warehouse / twoshot

n_removed:        How many requirements were removed for this variant (the delta)
n_omissions:      How many omission items the gap detector flagged (its total output)
n_additions:      How many addition items the gap detector flagged

omissions_match:   Detector omissions the judge matched to a tracked requirement with full MATCH quality
omissions_partial: Matched with PARTIAL quality (right area, imprecise description)
omissions_none:    No match — the FP_extra items, unverifiable against tracked requirements

reqs_found_via_omissions: Count of distinct removed requirements covered by at least one MATCH or PARTIAL omission item (broad; deduped)

And the same four columns repeated for additions (additions_match, additions_partial, additions_none, reqs_found_via_additions).

See precision_tracked.csv that uses the statistics in this file and in ground truth to compute precision
and recall.
